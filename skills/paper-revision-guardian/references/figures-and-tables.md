# Figure, Table, Caption and Placement Audit

Inspect actual rendered figures and table cells rather than relying on captions. Record what is visually observed, verified from data/code, merely inferred, or unavailable.

For figures check panel labels, axes, units, legends, line/style colors, bounds, curves, conditions, sample sizes where relevant, and consistency with manuscript claims. Captions identify the experiment, measured quantity and conditions concisely; they must not make unverified effectiveness claims. Explain necessary visual symbols and abbreviations. Review placement near first substantive mention while respecting journal floats and page layout; never promise 100% optimal placement.

For tables check numerical statements, best/worst, rankings, percentage changes, rounding, metric direction, dataset names, statistical markers, baseline comparability and units. Caption identifies what is compared; table notes clarify symbols and conditions; narrative interprets key evidence without duplicating every cell. Report mismatches with exact table/figure reference and proposed correction.

If image/data are missing, state precisely that the item could not be verified.
