# Detailed Workflow: Academic Paper Revision

[简体中文](WORKFLOW_ZH.md) · [Skill Home](../README_EN.md)

## Editorial position

This Skill is not a synonym-replacement prompt. It combines academic copyediting with cautious technical review. Scientific integrity, user scope and actual journal author instructions take precedence over style preferences. Revision should not normalize all prose into a generic AI-written academic register or inflate findings.

## Author-voice calibration

Read enough untouched manuscript sections to identify natural sentence lengths, syntax, terminology, voice, figure citation style, paragraph density and narrative rhythm. Retain already strong passages as written. Make local corrections only when grammar, ambiguity, repetition, readability or reasoning benefits.

## Contribution-led narrative and mechanism explanation

After establishing the authorial voice baseline, assess whether the problem, prior-work gap, specific contribution, principal mechanisms, findings, and implications form a consistent line of argument. This is a diagnostic framework, not a prescribed section layout or sentence template.

Review whether the core method explains the design motivation, defining concepts, interaction of components, and concrete differences from prior work. Identify development-log narration, unnecessary stage lists, promotional novelty language, and repetitive disclaimers that displace necessary explanations. Results should report observations and comparisons; discussion should interpret their meaning and boundaries rather than merely repeat the results. Align the scope of the title, abstract, contribution statement and conclusion.

Prefer local edits and retain clear original prose. After revision, compare vocabulary, syntax, paragraph rhythm, and research framing against the unchanged manuscript. Do not fabricate missing claims or evidence. This stage authorizes writing and argument review only; it does not authorize new experiments, data changes, statistical computation or submission operations.

See the [14-point writing policy](../references/manuscript-writing-and-revision.md) and [English examples](EXAMPLES_EN.md).

## Three issue classes

**A — Objective corrections:** confirmed spelling, grammar, citation and numerical mismatches; correct with evidence and record the location.

**B — Justified editing:** remove repetitive report-like stages, empty First/Second transitions, unnecessary defensive language and redundant figure descriptions when this improves coherence. Keep important limitations and procedural numbering where necessary.

**C — Consequential research questions:** mathematical claims, algorithm correctness, statistics, comparability and robustness/generalization assertions. Verify against data, code and derivations before any substantive rewrite. Mark unresolved questions for the author.

## Figures, tables and experiment claims

Inspect actual graphics and table contents, including axes, units, legend, subfigure identifiers, conditions and numerical comparisons. Captions identify the displayed material; text interprets key supported findings. Recalculate claimed percentages and rankings where source data exist. Assess placement relative to first substantive mention and compiled layout, not by an ungrounded promise of perfection.

## Equations, algorithms and LaTeX

Review symbols, dimensional consistency, assumptions, constraints, iteration order, stopping criteria and source-code agreement. Freeze unverified formulas. Preserve mathematical environments, citations, labels, macros and valid original formatting. Passing a static scan or compiling does not prove research correctness.

## Reviewer correspondence

Maintain a traceable link from each reviewer concern to evidence, changed manuscript location and truthful response. Never claim experiments were run without records or omit genuine concerns to make prose seem less defensive.

## Delivery

For a short passage, provide revised English, faithful Chinese translation and a concise Chinese modification/technical audit log. For a full editable project, preserve the original and deliver a diff or revised copy plus an audit report. Independently recheck every modified factual claim and report which evidence and checks were unavailable.
